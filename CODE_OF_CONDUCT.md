# Code of Conduct

Blogify is a small learning project, and everyone who takes part in it (in issues, pull requests, reviews and comments on the live site) should be able to do so without harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent, even when you disagree.
- Keep feedback about the code or the idea, not the person.
- Accept that the maintainer has the final say on what gets merged.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address or email, without their permission
- Spam, and deliberately disrupting discussions

## Reporting

Report problems to the maintainer, [@RoniRachmani](https://github.com/RoniRachmani). For abusive content on GitHub, you can also use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) option. Security problems go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block anyone who keeps doing so, temporarily or permanently.
